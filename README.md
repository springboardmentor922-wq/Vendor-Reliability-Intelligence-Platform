# VendorPulse

**Vendor Reliability Intelligence & Procurement Risk Management Platform**

VendorPulse is a procurement platform for managing vendors, procurement requests, purchase orders, deliveries, vendor performance, reliability, contracts, communication, notifications, and payments.

## Key Features

- Vendor Management
- Six Vendor Categories
- Procurement Requests & Vendor Assignment
- Vendor Acceptance
- Purchase Order Management
- Delivery Tracking
- Vendor Performance & Reliability
- Contract & Compliance Management
- Notifications & Communication
- Finance & Payment Management
- Reports & Analytics
- Role-Based Access Control

## Vendor Categories

- Raw Material Suppliers
- Equipment Vendors
- IT Vendors
- Service Providers
- Logistics Partners
- Maintenance Vendors

## User Roles

Administrator | Procurement Manager | Supply Chain Manager | Vendor Manager | Vendor | Finance Officer | Auditor

## Technology

**Python • Streamlit • FastAPI • MongoDB • JWT • RBAC**
