# VendorIQ User Manual

## 1. Sign in

Open the VendorIQ login page and use an enterprise account. The local demo accounts use the shared password `VendorIQ@2026`.

After authentication, VendorIQ routes the user to a role-appropriate starting workspace.

## 2. Navigate

The left navigation shows only areas available to the current role. On smaller screens it becomes a drawer. The top bar provides global search, notifications and the signed-in user menu.

## 3. Vendors

Use **Vendors** to review supplier status, category, contact details and risk state. Authorized users can add suppliers, approve pending vendors and upload supporting evidence.

Open approvals only after checking the supplier record. Approval actions are written to the audit trail.

## 4. Procurement

Create a procurement request with requirement, quantity, department, priority, budget and required date. Authorized managers can approve or reject the request.

## 5. Purchase orders

Create a PO by selecting an approved vendor and one or more line items. Each line captures item, quantity, unit price and tax. Review subtotal, tax and total before saving.

The lifecycle includes pending, approved, ordered, delivered, completed and cancelled/rejected outcomes. Vendor users can acknowledge approved orders and mark their own delivered orders.

## 6. Performance

Operational scorecards capture delivery, quality, cost, communication, service and issue-resolution ratings. VendorIQ calculates reliability from the stored values rather than a hardcoded dashboard number.

## 7. Reliability & Risk

The historical intelligence workspace is explicitly labeled as DataCo supplier-proxy intelligence. Product-level records are not presented as real vendor identities. Open a scorecard to see factor contributions and recommendations.

## 8. Contracts & Compliance

Use filters and date cues to find active, expiring, expired, compliant and non-compliant agreements. Renewal and compliance state are shown alongside the vendor.

## 9. Invoices

Finance users can record PO-linked invoices, approve them and mark them paid. The invoice summary reports open value, pending records, overdue records and settled value.

## 10. Communications

Open a vendor thread to inspect message history. New communications record sender, subject, vendor, thread and timestamp. The workspace is designed as a structured supplier relationship record rather than a consumer chat.

## 11. Notifications

Use the alert center to read/unread operational notifications. Authorized managers can run an alert scan for overdue deliveries, contract expiry, compliance and invoice conditions.

## 12. Reports

Preview live report rows before exporting to CSV, Excel or PDF. Exports are generated from current database state.

## 13. Audit

Auditors and authorized governance roles can inspect recorded mutations including vendor creation/approval, PO approvals, contract changes and invoice lifecycle events.

## 14. Profile

Use **Profile & Settings** to change the display name and review the active environment/session information.
